Story 1031 (a Nature paper, reader-requested) ran four write turns on prompts of 1902 → 5825 → 9598 → 14435 tokens in 49s → 69s → 111s → 153s, then spent the full 600s cap on the fifth and died as `ReadTimeout`, leaving no post. Prompt eval fell from 143 to 15.8 tok/s across the run; at that rate the fifth turn's ~16.6k-token prompt needs ~1000s of prompt processing before a single token is generated, so the cap could not have been met. A game took the GPU partway through (09:29:19, before the fourth turn ended) and made the last turn hopeless, but **the decay from 35 to 1.3 tok/s happened before the game started** and is the real finding: `main` is a 21.7 GB 35B MoE on a 10 GB card, so every turn on a growing prompt is CPU-bound. The mechanism is untouched, so the next reader-requested story that fails does this again.
